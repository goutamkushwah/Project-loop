Project - Loop# 🚀 Project LOOP – AI Customer Feedback Intelligence Platform

Project LOOP is an AI-powered Customer Feedback Intelligence Platform that helps businesses collect, organize, analyze, and understand customer feedback from multiple sources in one centralized dashboard.

Instead of manually reading hundreds of customer reviews, support tickets, survey responses, and feedback comments, Project LOOP uses Artificial Intelligence to automatically classify sentiment, identify recurring themes, detect emerging trends, and generate actionable business insights.

The platform is designed as a secure multi-tenant SaaS application where multiple organizations can manage their customer feedback independently while maintaining complete data isolation.

# 📖 Overview

Modern businesses receive customer feedback from many different channels including:

- Support Tickets
- Product Reviews
- App Store Reviews
- Survey Responses
- Sales Notes
- Social Media Comments

Managing all this feedback manually is difficult.

Project LOOP solves this problem by collecting all feedback into one platform and using Artificial Intelligence to transform raw feedback into meaningful insights.

The platform helps product managers, support teams, and business leaders understand:

- What customers like
- What customers dislike
- Which features are requested most
- Which issues are increasing over time
- What improvements should be prioritized

---

# ❓ Problem Statement

Companies often receive thousands of feedback messages every month.

Reading every message manually is:

- Time consuming
- Error prone
- Difficult to organize
- Impossible to scale

As a result, valuable customer insights are often lost.

Project LOOP automates this entire process using AI.
